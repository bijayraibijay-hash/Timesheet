The app keeps a copy each day (last 7 days) and one before every restore. They protect against mistakes, but they are lost if the app’s data is cleared, so keep making real backups too.

Share this app
Send the app to anyone with an iPhone or Android phone. iPhone: open the link in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Android: open it in Chrome, tap the menu (three dots), then Install app or Add to Home screen. Your shifts and pay are never shared.
💼 Jobs
Each job keeps its own shifts, pay rate, penalty and overtime rules, leave balances, pay period and reports. Backups include every job.
Pay settings & backup
Pay is an estimate before tax. Enter the extra % for each penalty type (for example 50 for time and a half), then choose the pay type on each shift. Always check your payslip.
Pay rules, tax & super
These settings belong to the job you are viewing. Saturday, Sunday and public holiday rates are in Pay settings above.

Tax & super
Overtime counts shifts only. If an overtime hour is also on a weekend or public holiday, the higher rate is used. Night loading is added on top. These are estimates, so check your award and payslip.
